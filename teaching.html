Teaching Philosophy

I believe that meaningful learning begins with genuine connection. I learn every student's name, encourage them to call me by name, and praise even their smallest contributions. By offering constructive feedback and creating a space where students feel seen and valued, I aim to encourage them to participate more in the class. I draw on my own experience as a student when designing my lessons. I create lesson content that resonates with students' lives. For instance, I use social media discourse to explore language and rhetoric. This not only captures their interest but also helps them develop a critical perspective and attention to their daily language use. Informed by the Zone of Proximal Development, I structure learning in a step-by-step manner. I break complex assignments into manageable stages, provide examples for each step for thorough comprehension, and integrate group activities that build teamwork and analytical thinking. My main goal is to equip students with problem-solving and collaborative skills they will carry into their academic and professional lives.
