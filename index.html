Welcome!

Ismail Berkand Gök

Applied Linguistics & TESL · Northern Arizona University

I am a Graduate Teaching Assistant and MA student in Applied Linguistics and Teaching English as a Second Language program at Northern Arizona University (Flagstaff, AZ). A few examples of my research interests are corpus linguistics, discourse analysis (especially, gaming and social media discouse discourse), and game-based learning.

I also hold a BA in English Language Teaching from Middle East Technical University (Ankara, Türkiye), where I graduated as a High Honor student. I served as President of the METU Science Fiction and Fantasy Society, organizing conventions and workshops that sparked people's interest in alternative cultures, such as tabletop roleplaying games (TTRPGs), video games, cosplay, and board games.

Outside academia, I worked as a freelance fantasy map designer and tabletop role-playing game story writer, connecting my love of game design interests with my scholarly interests.
